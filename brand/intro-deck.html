INTRODUCTION For car clubs, partners and investors
Why B-Roads

Navigation apps are built to get you there fast. Enthusiasts want the drive to be the point.

Built for commuters

Mainstream maps send everyone down the fastest road: motorways, toll roads and traffic. The winding country roads that make a drive memorable never get suggested.

Group drives run on chat threads

Clubs organise convoys through messaging groups and shared pins. Cars split at junctions, someone always takes a wrong turn, and no one knows who has arrived.

Organising falls on one person

Dates, sign-ups, routes and headcounts end up scattered across chats and spreadsheets. Nobody keeps a record of the good roads or the drives the club has done.

B-Roads is built around the drive itself, not just the destination.

Meet B-Roads

Think of it as Waze for driving enthusiasts, focused on three things done well.

Find the back roads

Choose where you're starting and where you're heading. B-Roads avoids motorways and toll roads, and picks out the twisty, scenic routes worth driving.

Drive together

Start a group drive and share a code. Everyone sees the group live on the map, and each driver's time is recorded. Pit stop photos post straight to Instagram or Facebook.

Run your club PREMIUM

Club events with sign-ups, convoy tools that keep everyone together, a library of tested routes and a history of every club drive.

B-ROADSThe solution
Feature one

Routes picked for the drive, not the commute.

  • Set a start and a destination. Search any place, or start from where you are now.

  • Motorways and tolls are avoided. B-Roads favours back roads and only uses big roads as a last resort.

  • Every route option is rated. Each alternative is scored on how twisty it is and how much of it stays on back roads. The best one is flagged Most fun.

  • Pick your mood. Relaxed for a scenic cruise, Balanced for most days, or Wild for the twistiest roads around.

  • Turn-by-turn guidance. The next turn is always on screen, with a warning if you leave the planned route.

B-ROADSBack-road routing
Feature two

Group drives without the chaos.

  • Create a drive. The organiser picks the route and gets a short drive code.

  • Share the code or invite link. Members join from the meeting point with one tap.

  • Drive together, live. Everyone sees every car on the map and how far each driver has left to go. If someone loses signal, they rejoin automatically.

  • Arrive together. When everyone is in, the drive summary shows how long the group took. It's a shared drive, not a race: nobody is ranked.

  • Share the stop. Snap a pit stop photo stamped with where you are, and post it to Instagram or Facebook in one tap. Free for everyone.

Feature three · Premium

Everything a club organiser needs, in one place.

  • Club events and sign-ups. Post a drive with a date, meeting point and route. Members reply going, maybe or can’t make it, so you know who’s coming.

  • Convoy tools. Name a lead car and a back-marker, set regroup points along the route, and get an alert the moment a car falls behind.

  • Route library. Save the club’s best tested roads with notes, so anyone can drive them again.

  • Club drive history. Every club drive is logged: the route, who came and how long it took.

Where we are and where we're heading

In development today, with a clear road ahead.

Today

B-Roads is in development for iPhone and Android. The core features are being built now: back-road routing, live group drives, pit stop sharing and club organiser tools. Road testing with drivers comes next.

Next on the roadmap

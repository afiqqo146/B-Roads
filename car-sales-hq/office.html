How it's built

Five steps

  1. 1

    One agent, one job

    Each agent is a short brief: who it is, its one job and its hard limits. .claude/agents/

  2. 2

    Only the tools it needs

    Writers can only edit files. Three agents can search the web. No agent can send, post or pay.

  3. 3

    The shared brain

    One folder every agent reads and writes: customers, stock, promos, the board, the outbox. brain/

  4. 4

    Leads + sub-agents

    The Front Desk runs each playbook, starting independent agents side by side. .claude/skills/

  5. 5

    Human in the loop

    Messages, posts, quotes and prices land on your desk as drafts. /approve goes through them.

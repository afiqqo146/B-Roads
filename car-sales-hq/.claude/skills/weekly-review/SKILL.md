---
name: weekly-review
description: Coach's Corner playbook. Weekly scorecard, pipeline health and one thing to practise. Use on Fridays or when the user asks how they're doing.
---

Run **at the same time**:
- **numbers-tracker**: the month's scorecard and pace vs target.
- **board-keeper**: list STUCK cards and leads with no next step.
- **incentive-tracker**: promos ending next week.

Then reply with: the scorecard, the 3 deals most worth pushing next week and why, and one objection to practise. Offer: "Want to role-play it? Say the word and I'll bring in the objection-coach."
